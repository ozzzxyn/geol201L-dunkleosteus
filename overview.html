Overview

The Dunkleosteus is an extinct species of the "Arthrodire" fish which existed during the Late Devonian period, around 382-358 million years ago. It was a fish that primarily lived in open waters, and is considered to be one of the first vertebrate apex predators of any ecosystem at the time.

It's fossils were found in different regions of the world which include, the United States, Canada, Poland, Belgium, and Morocco. It's fossil record history will be covered more in detail here.

This fish had multiple different species, those being the D. terrelli, D. belgicus, D. denisoni, D. marsaisi, D. magnificus, D. missouriensis, D. newberryi, D. amblyodoratus, D. raveri, and D. tuderensis. I won't be covering them all but I thought I should mention them all.

It's body size isn't known for certain, but estimates for the biggest species have estimated between 3.1 to 10 m, for visual above that's 8.8m or about 29 ft. These estimations seem to be based on the size of the skull as it's body hasn't been commonly recovered.
