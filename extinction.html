Extinction

The Dunkleosteus had disappeared during the end of the Devonian Period. During the Late Devonian. Though as sudden as it sounds, it was a series of extinctions that happened over millions of years.

Scientists believed the extinction was due to a combination of environmental changes rather than just one cause. The ocean had lost oxygen, through a something called anoxia. These large parts of the oceans had become low in oxygen, leaving lots of different marine animals not surviving this low of a oxygen level. Even ecosystems that were mainly bottom-dwelling had collapsed which in turn, affects the survival of the Dunkleosteus.



Climate Change

The Devonian climate began to cool over time, with Glaciation (ice formation) possibly occuring in this time. Climate change such as this affects the sea level, leading to it dropping with shallow habitats shrinking. The reefs of the Devonian were some of the largest reefs in all of Earth's history with them collapsing because of the major change in climate. Food webs broke down and many predators lost their sources to prey on and survive.

Though the expansion of earth forests increased, soil began to form and nutrients expanded into the oceans. Causing possible algal blooms, and further worsened the oxygen in the ocean.



Survival

The Dunkleosteus as said before, didn't survive the previously described climate change as it affected many species that were alive during this time. In order for the Dunkleosteus to survive, it needed large amounts of food at a time, heavily depending on stable marine ecosystems with shallow waters heavily being affected. Less prey mean't less food to prey on.
