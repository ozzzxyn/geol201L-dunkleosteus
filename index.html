The Armored Giant of the Ancient Seas

Dunkleosteus terrelli was a massive placoderm fish that dominated the oceans roughly 380 million years ago during the Late Devonian period. With self-sharpening bony jaw plates and a body up to 20 feet long, it was one of Earth's first great apex predators.
